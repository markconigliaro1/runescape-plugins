# Preset Vault

The game gives you 15 bank presets. Preset Vault keeps as many more as you
like, so you can overwrite a preset slot without screenshotting the old one
first.

- **Open it from your bank:** a **Preset Vault** button appears above your
  bank's items whenever the bank is open. Press it again, or the **X**, to
  put the window away.
- **Save** what you're carrying and wearing, and what your beast of burden is
  carrying if one is summoned, under a name you choose.
- **Browse** your saved setups in a list. The selected one is shown as item
  icons, laid out like your backpack, worn equipment and beast of burden.
- **Show what's missing:** items you already have fade out, so the ones you
  still need stand out. With your bank open, the items you still need are
  outlined in green in your bank, and you're told if your bank doesn't have
  enough of something.
- **Spot layout mistakes:** while showing what's missing, any backpack, worn
  or beast of burden slot holding the wrong thing is framed: red if the
  preset doesn't use that item, orange if it's the right item in the wrong
  slot. In the bank,
  the same slots are outlined in the bank's view of your backpack.
- **Move the window:** click the **::** grip next to the X in its title bar,
  move your mouse, and click the grip again to drop it. It remembers where you
  left it.

Preset Vault is a reference only. It never withdraws, equips or loads
anything for you: you rebuild the setup yourself, then save it to a real
preset slot if you want.

## Settings

Use the tick box in the Preset Vault window, above the name box.

- **Strict slot order** (on by default): flags preset items that are in the
  wrong backpack or beast of burden slot. Turn it off if you only care that
  you have the right items, not where they sit; items the preset doesn't use
  are still flagged.

## Permissions

None.

## Data protection

Preset Vault stores your saved setups (item IDs and quantities, and the names
you give them) and the window's position, using the client's plugin storage.
It stores nothing about other players and sends nothing anywhere.

## Example

1. Open your bank and press **Preset Vault**.
2. Gear up for a boss, type "Vorago" in the name box and press **Save**.
3. Later, overwrite that preset slot in game with something else.
4. When you want the old setup back, select "Vorago", press
   **Show what's missing** and open your bank. Withdraw and equip the items
   that are still solid until everything has faded.
